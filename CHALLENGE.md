# Challenge B: make a motor claim easier under stress

Med Assurance is the challenge case name. Its corporate identity and affiliations have not been verified. All customers, policies and claims in this starter are fictional, and no affiliation with the listed providers is implied. A Moroccan motorist has just had an accident. Policy details, contacts, documents and claim history are scattered, so the motorist repeats information and cannot see the next step. A broker employee needs one reliable view without treating an automated suggestion as a coverage or liability decision.

The starter works, but its experience is deliberately poor. Choose one audience and one bounded outcome. Research at least two relevant product patterns, explain which interaction you adopt, then implement and test a complete improvement.

Possible paths:

- help an injured or uncertain motorist reach the correct human action before form completion;
- reduce anxiety and re-entry across draft, review, submission and tracking;
- make broker history, provenance, conflicts and next actions understandable;
- turn the simulated communication assistant into a safe, efficient review workflow;
- improve interrupted, duplicate and conflicting browser-import recovery.

Your solution must keep all fixtures fictional and local. It must not claim to determine coverage, liability or compensation. Keep the ACAPS references next to process guidance, distinguish general service from emergency assistance, and preserve human review before any simulated client update.

Demonstrate keyboard and mobile use, loading/empty/error states where relevant, one failed attempt and the correction, and a resettable path. Record remaining mocks and assumptions. A polished screenshot without a working journey is incomplete.

Deliver a working demo, source without secrets or dependency caches, setup instructions, a short audience/problem statement, before-and-after evidence, two reference links with reasoning, test notes and a known-gap list.
