# Sources and provenance

Checked 19 September 2026. The source pages may change; verify current details before operational use.

- [ACAPS driver kit](https://www.acaps.ma/sites/default/files/2025-04/ACAPS_Kit%20du%20conducteur%20FR.pdf): injury and official-report escalation context.
- [ACAPS material-damage steps](https://www.acaps.ma/sites/default/files/vf_step_degats_materiels_accidentvoiture.pdf): prompt notification, evidence and insurer-led review. The general five-day motor example is not implemented as a deadline calculator.
- [Sanlam motor accident assistance](https://sanlam.ma/fr/sinistres/accident-de-voiture/): 3434 and +212 5 22 95 75 75, identified as 24/7 motor assistance.
- [RMA contact](https://www.rmaassurance.com/fr/nous-contacter): 2526, general customer relations.
- [Wafa Assurance contact](https://www.wafaassurance.ma/fr/compagnie-assurance-maroc/contact): +212 5 22 54 55 55 from the official search index; direct page availability failed during the check.
- [AXA Assurance Maroc](https://www.axa.ma/): official reference only; no phone retained because official variants disagreed.
- [TRT Broker legal notice](https://trtbroker.com/mentions-legales), [login](https://trtbroker.com/compte/connexion) and [registration](https://trtbroker.com/compte/inscription): verified broker identity and public entry points.
- [OuiAssur](https://www.ouiassur.ma/), [login](https://www.ouiassur.ma/login) and [registration](https://www.ouiassur.ma/register): ALINQAD ASSURANCE broker identity and public entry points.
- [Digiassur FAQ](https://fr.digiassur.ma/FAQ): private-space claim and document functions; credentials described as post-subscription.
- [MesAssurances](https://www.mesassurances.ma/): explicitly a technology platform, neither insurer nor broker.
- [GOV.UK question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/) and [check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/): usability inspiration only, not Moroccan insurance authority.

All names, policy numbers, claims, vehicles, task histories and import outcomes in `data.js` were authored as synthetic fixtures for this challenge. The interface makes no network request.
